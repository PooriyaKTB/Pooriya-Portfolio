Event-Driven Job Scheduler

A cron scheduler where deciding when a job runs and actually running it are two separate parts of the system. Quartz reads the cron expression and fires at the right moment, but instead of doing the work itself, the scheduler publishes an event to Kafka. Independent consumers pick those events up and execute the jobs.

Splitting scheduling from execution means each side can scale and fail on its own. I'm now working on retries and failure handling when a consumer drops a job halfway through.

ThisAbility (EquiVerse)

A journey planner for people with disabilities, built around one question: what would make someone confident enough to travel independently? Our team started with interviews, surveys and a review of 7+ existing products, and used that to decide what to build first. I then built the interactive prototype and the project website. Routes come from the TfL Journey Planner, with GraphHopper and OSRM as fallbacks, so the app still finds a way when one service is down.

From user interviews to a working prototype, pitched to judges at Google London.

PiTranslate

A translator built for learning, not just translating. Every result comes with a pronunciation guide, example sentences, synonyms and a matching idiom, and misspelled input gets a "did you mean" fix. Saved words come back on a spaced-repetition schedule: get one right and the gap before the next review doubles, miss it and it returns tomorrow. The Express backend keeps the OpenAI key off the client and handles rate limiting and input validation.

Lighthouse performance score of 95+ after tuning rendering and API calls.
